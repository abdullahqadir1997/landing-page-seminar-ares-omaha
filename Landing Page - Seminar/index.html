A decade of jiu-jitsu, friendship & community.

For the past 10 years, Ares BJJ Omaha has been more than just a gym. It's been a place where friendships were built, confidence was developed, challenges were overcome, and lives were transformed on the mats.

Now we're inviting YOU to celebrate this incredible milestone with us — whether you're a longtime student, brand new to Jiu-Jitsu, or simply someone who loves being part of a great community.

This event is for you.
